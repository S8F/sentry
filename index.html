The problem

Screen time is the tax on this strategy.

The good entries at a support or resistance level are rare — one or two a day — and they resolve in minutes. Miss the bar, miss the trade.

So the strategy quietly demands six hours of staring to catch ninety seconds of opportunity. Most people can't. The ones who can end up trading tired, taking mediocre setups to justify the time already spent.

Sentry does the staring. You get the ninety seconds.

How it works

Three moves, repeated all session.

Before the open

It marks your levels

You hand it the prices that matter today — the supports, the resistances, the line that flips the day bearish. It puts each one on the chart and stands there.

All session

It grades what shows up

No fixed pattern list. When price reaches a level, Sentry reads the tape across seven timeframes and scores what it sees — A+ down to C — on the weight of everything at once, never one signal.

On the good ones

It pings your wrist

Top grades only. Phone and Apple Watch. The ticket is already drawn on the chart — entry, stop, targets — so the decision takes seconds, not analysis.

Guardrails

The rules that keep it honest.

  • No single vetoNo one indicator can kill a setup on its own. The score is the weight of all the evidence together — the way a person actually reads a chart.
  • No chasingIt rests a limit at the edge of structure. If price never comes back to it, there is no trade. Entering late on a confirmed close is not allowed.
  • Both sides, alwaysEvery level is graded long and short. The agent is never given a directional opinion to defend.
  • A watchdog on topA second, read-only agent checks each read as it happens — and blocks the launch outright if the day's levels are stale.
  • It never places an orderSentry draws and alerts. A human presses the button. Always.

Under the hood

Not one model in a loop — a team with a referee.

Status, plainly

It has never traded real money.

Sentry is in testing. Everything above is built and running daily on live market data, but no trade it has ever produced was taken with real capital, and there is no track record to show you.

What exists is the system, the guardrails, and the test harness that scores it honestly — including on the days it fails. That is the part worth looking at right now.
